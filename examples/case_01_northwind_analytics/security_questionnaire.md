# Security Questionnaire -- Northwind Analytics

**Q: Is customer data encrypted at rest?**
A: Yes.

**Q: Is MFA enforced for all employee access to production systems?**
A: Yes.

**Q: Do you have a documented, tested incident response plan?**
A: Not formally, no -- we handle incidents as they come up. We don't currently have a written runbook, but our engineering team is experienced and responsive.

**Q: Are all subprocessors disclosed to customers?**
A: Yes.

**Q: What SOC 2 coverage do you have?**
A: Type II report available on request.

**Q: What is your committed customer notification window in the event of a data breach?**
A: We aim to notify affected customers within 24 hours of confirming an incident.
